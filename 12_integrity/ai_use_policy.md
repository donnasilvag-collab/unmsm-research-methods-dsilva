# AI use policy for this research repository

## Scope

This policy applies to the preparation of course deliverables and research materials in this repository. It covers generative AI and other automated assistance used for drafting, editing, code support, summarization, or documentation.

## Permitted use

AI tools may support language editing, document organization, code explanation, reproducibility checks, and the preparation of non-sensitive public documentation. The author remains responsible for the research question, methodological choices, citations, data interpretation, and final wording.

## Prohibited input

Do not submit raw survey responses, interview recordings or transcripts, participant identifiers, contact details, credentials, proprietary source code, confidential organizational documents, security configurations, vulnerability details, or incident records to an external AI service unless the required authorization and data-processing assessment are complete.

## Verification and disclosure

AI output must be reviewed against the source material before it is accepted. It must not introduce citations that the author has not checked, make claims that the evidence does not support, or replace the author's academic judgment. When AI contributed to a document, the document should include a concise disclosure that describes the type of support provided.

## Record of responsibility

The author, Donna Silva, is accountable for all material published in this repository. This policy complements the confidentiality rules in `09_ethics/ethics_protocol.md` and the data-handling controls in `10_data_mgmt/data_management_plan.md`.
